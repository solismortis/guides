Back up your personal data

In W11 use MiniTool Partition Wizard to create 3 approximately equal in size partitions (1 for W11, 1 for Ubuntu (ext4), 1 for shared data (ntfs; where you should keep most of your personal data [not Telegram data, though, as it struggles accessing it from Ubuntu])). Don't accidentally erase W11 here

Use rufus to create a U22 USD drive (default all settings)

Disable Fast Boot, hibernation and BitLocker in W11

Plug in the USD drive. Turn off PC

Start your PC and start hitting F2 or DELETE fast to get into BIOS (or some other key depending on your machine)

Boot into the Ubuntu USD drive from the menu

Choose "Try Ubuntu", play around a bit, get Internet connection

Start the installation. This is where you can accidentally fuck up and erase your Windows. Get to the "Something else" radio button, select it and proceed. Choose the ext4 as root (the "/" symbol) and make sure to check "format" (yes, we just created it, but if you don't check it, you may experience issues). Don't touch other partitions. Install Ubuntu

It is likely that W11 won't give up its control, so you will have to get into BIOS again, find UEFI and give Ubuntu grub control

Done. It is possible that some W11 update will take over control again. In that case, repeat the prev step
